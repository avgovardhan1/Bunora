Golden outside. Molten inside.

Bunora started with one idea: a bun that gives you something warm the moment you bite it.

How every bun is made

  1. Slow proofingThe dough rests overnight, which gives the bun its soft, airy crumb.
  2. Baked until goldenA hot oven crisps the crust while the inside stays pillowy.
  3. Filled when you orderChocolate or pistachio goes in last, so it is still flowing when you cut in.
